In this horde survival game with rhythm mechanics, you survive by dodging enemies and defeating them with the power of music! With each swing, your weapons create sounds that tear into enemies and make the soundtrack richer as you grow more powerful. Tap to the beat in the right way and you can cast spells that make you even stronger!
